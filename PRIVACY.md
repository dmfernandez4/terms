# Privacy Policy: Personal Finance Monitor

Last updated: 24 October 2026

## Who operates this application
Personal Finance Monitor is a personal, non-commercial application operated by <Your Full Name>
(contact: <your-email@example.com>). It has a single user: the operator.

## What data is accessed
With the operator's explicit consent, given through their bank's own authentication via
Enable Banking (PSD2 account information service), the application reads only:
- account identifiers (name, IBAN, currency, account type)
- account balances
- transaction history (date, amount, description, counterparty)

Only bank accounts that the operator owns and has linked are accessed.

## How data is used
The data is used solely to show the operator their own balances, spending categories and
transfers between their own accounts. No profiling, advertising, credit scoring or
automated decision-making is performed.

## Where data is stored
All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer. No cloud database, analytics,
tracking or third-party services are used, apart from Enable Banking, which provides the bank
connection.

## Sharing
Data is not sold, shared or disclosed to any third party.

## Retention
Data is kept for as long as the operator chooses. Bank access consent expires automatically
(at most 180 days) and can be revoked at any time from the application or the bank. Deleting
the local data files removes all stored data.

## Your rights
Because the operator is the only data subject, they control their data fully, including access,
correction, deletion and withdrawal of consent, in line with the EU General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR). Questions: <your-email@example.com>.
